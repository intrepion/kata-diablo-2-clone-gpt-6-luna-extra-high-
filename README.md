# Ashen Wilds

A small, original browser action RPG inspired by classic isometric dungeon crawlers. Roam the Blood Moor, hunt demons, collect gear, and defeat the Bloodhorn.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step, server, or third-party assets are required.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrows:** move
- **Mouse click:** move to ground or lock on to an enemy
- **Q:** Ember Bolt
- **E:** Whirlwind
- **Space:** evade roll
- **R:** drink a healing potion
- **F:** pick up nearby loot
- **I:** open inventory
- **Esc:** close a panel or pause

Defeat twelve demons to draw out the Bloodhorn. Defeated enemies grant experience and may drop gold or equipment. Equip found gear from the inventory panel.

The game is a compact playable slice rather than a full multi-act remake: it has one wilderness map, one hero archetype, three enemy types, an elite boss, loot, equipment, and level progression.
